# Licensing Overview

TrustWeave is distributed under a dual-license model managed by [Geoknoesis LLC](https://www.geoknoesis.com): the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0), or a commercial licence.

## Open Source Licence (AGPL-3.0)

- **Who can use it:** Anyone, for any purpose, including commercial and production use, provided they comply with the AGPL-3.0 (see `LICENSE`).
- **Rights granted:** Use, modification and redistribution of the source code.
- **Obligations:** If you distribute TrustWeave or a work based on it, or let users interact with a modified version over a network, you must make the corresponding source code available to them under the AGPL-3.0.

## Geoknoesis Commercial Licence

- **Who needs it:** Anyone who wants to use TrustWeave in proprietary software or services without the AGPL-3.0's source-sharing obligations, or who wants commercial support terms.
- **Benefits:** Commercial licence agreements include enterprise support, roadmap collaboration, and optional customizations tailored to your deployment needs.
- **How to obtain:** Contact the Geoknoesis team via [www.geoknoesis.com](https://www.geoknoesis.com) or email `contact@geoknoesis.com` to discuss licensing tiers and support packages.

## Choosing the Right Licence

| Scenario | Licence |
|----------|---------|
| University lab building proofs-of-concept | AGPL-3.0 |
| Open-source project released under an AGPL-compatible licence | AGPL-3.0 |
| Company that publishes its TrustWeave-based service's source under the AGPL-3.0 | AGPL-3.0 |
| Proprietary product or closed-source SaaS built on TrustWeave | Commercial |
| Enterprise wanting support, warranties or custom terms | Commercial |

This page is a summary, not legal advice; the licence texts govern.
